## 4. Financial Communities & Trading Forums (Traffic & Backlinks)

Participate and share links to your daily blog posts to drive immediate organic traffic.

*   **TradingView Ideas**:
    *   Create a profile for `aigoldforecast.com`.
    *   Publish chart analysis screenshots and include links to your daily blog reports for the full technical breakdown.
*   **Reddit (r/Gold, r/trading, r/Forex)**:
    *   Share high-value content (e.g., your **Pillar Post** explaining how you trained the OHLC model).
    *   *Rule of thumb*: Focus on the technical education first; only place a link back to the terminal at the very end of the post.
*   **Medium / Hackernoon / LinkedIn**:
    *   Cross-post your daily market reports and technical articles.
    *   Use the "canonical link" setting on Medium pointing to your original blog URL (`https://aigoldforecast.com/blog/your-slug`) so Google doesn't mark it as duplicate content.
